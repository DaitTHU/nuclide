# NUBASE2020 data attribution

The bundled nuclear data are adapted from:

F.G. Kondev, M. Wang, W.J. Huang, S. Naimi, and G. Audi,
**The NUBASE2020 evaluation of nuclear physics properties**,
Chinese Physics C **45**, 030001 (2021).
DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1088/1674-1137/abddae>.

and the [final published article hosted by AMDC](https://amdc.impcas.ac.cn/masstables/Ame2020/Kondev_2021_Chinese_Phys._C_45_030001.pdf).
This attribution and the data license are separate from nuclide's software license.
The authors, AMDC, and IAEA do not endorse this package.

Source: <https://nds.iaea.org/amdc/ame2020/nubase_4.mas20.txt>

Retrieved: 2026-11-16.

SHA-256 of the uncompressed source:
`1585a5eea86c5e17e90307c7e6e786d060049c4039e392a261ff6db977df9859`.

Changes made by nuclide: selected ground states and the first two isomer labels
(`m`, `n`); omitted entries marked as reported nonexistent or existence uncertain;
normalized names and numeric fields; converted time units to seconds using the
evaluation's tropical year (31,556,926 seconds); preserved source qualifiers and
original rows. Decay-mode strings are retained without inferring daughter states
or a branching network. Uncertainties are not propagated.

`nubase_4.mas20.txt.gz` is a lossless compressed copy of the source.
`nubase2020.json` is generated by `tools/import_nubase.py` in the source repository.
The JSON metadata includes counts, excluded entries, and source provenance.
